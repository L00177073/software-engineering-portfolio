Software engineering week 1 reflection 


Software is a set of instructions that is used by certain hardware to perform a specific required task, unlike hardware software is not physical and it instructs the hardware on how to operate. Software is split into two main categories, System software which acts as the mainframe of the hardware (operating system, drivers) and then there is Application software which usually uses the hardware for more specialized tasks.

Software engineering was a necessary evolution of old school programming, back in the day software was less complicated but as time went on software became more prevalent in our daily lives and software started becoming more complicated and required large teams of programmers to develop, thus problems with the software such as bugs and errors started becoming more and more prevalent with time and it was decided that a more formal and structured way was needed to develop complicated software efficiently and at a lower cost thus software engineering was created. Software engineering combines various design techniques and models along with computing theories and techniques to deliver functional software systems on time and on budget.

During my third year of computing me and my team where tasked to develop a cinema website for bookings. Our initial and most frequent hurdle was with the database. Its initial conception was simple enough but as we developed the functionalities of the website we encountered a myriad of problems such as the database having errors with columns and everyone constantly making changes without communicating with the rest of the team, i nthe end we were able to come up with a final plan for the database and stick with it, but due to poor team communication work slowed down significantly. Another major problem we encountered during our team project development was the usage of GitHub for version control, we broke the repository due to our inexperience of using GitHub and lost a significant amount of our progress.
