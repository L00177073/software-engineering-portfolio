**Week 8 — Research Lab**

Aim
Design a small measurement approach for one external attribute (reliability, maintainability, or usability) for a
given application, and critique its limitations.

**Tasks**

**Task 1 — Pick an Attribute and an App**

1. Usability measures how easily users can learn, understand and use an application to complete their tasks effectively and efficiently.

2. Spotify
   
Spotify is an application I use regularly to listen to music, create playlists and search for songs. It is a suitable application for measuring usability because it involves common user tasks that can be timed and evaluated.

3. Measurement 1: System Usability Scale (SUS) score

   It measures the overall user satisfaction and perceived ease of use.
   I will collect the data by completing 10 standard SUS questionnaire statements after using Spotify. The rating will be from 1 to to 5.
   The collection period will be one week where the user after one week will complete the questionnaire.
   The expected results will be a score out of 100 with higher scores indicating better perceived usability.

   Measurement 2: Time-on-task

   It measures how long it takes a user to complete a specific task.
   The collection will happen by using a stopwatch to measure how long it takes to find a specific song using the search function and how long it takes
   to create a new playlist with at least five songs.
   The collection period would consist of repeating each task five times over five days and record the times in a spreadsheet.
   The expected results would be to calculate the average completion time for each task and the shorter completion times would indicate better usability.

**Task 2 Critique Your Own Approach**

  1. Potential problems with measurements
   One problem with my approach is that the time-on-task measurement could be misleading because I already use Spotify regularly.
   I am familiar with the layout and features, so I might complete tasks much faster than a new user. This could make Spotify appear easier to use than it actually is.
   Another issue is that completing the SUS questionnaire myself could introduce personal bias.

  2. Proposed improvement
     To make the measurements more trustworthy, I would ask five people who have different levels of experience with Spotify to complete the same tasks.

     I would:

       -Give everyone the same instructions and tasks.

       -Record their completion times using the same method.

       -Ask each participant to complete the SUS questionnaire afterwards.

       -Calculate the average task completion time and SUS score across all participants.

      This would provide a more balanced view of Spotify's usability because the results would include different user experiences rather than relying only on my own opinion.
